Awards

The Rising Star of The North

This special award recognizes the emerging Amateur soloist, couple, or duo with the highest score across the Showcase Competitions.

Award includes:

  • Special Recognition
  • Featured Promotion Across The North Latin Festival Platforms
  • Featured Performance at The North Latin Festival 2026 Gala Showcase

The North Top School

This award recognizes the dance school or company with the strongest overall performance across the Showcase Competitions. Schools will be ranked based on their competitors' performance throughout the competition, following an overall medal standings system.

Award includes:

  • Special Recognition
  • Featured Promotion Across The North Latin Festival Platforms
  • Contract Opportunity for The North Latin Festival 2027
