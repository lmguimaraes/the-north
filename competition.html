Important Notes
  • All competitors must hold a valid festival pass.
  • Competition fees are non-refundable.
  • Registration is only confirmed once the Podium registration and payment receipt have been submitted and verified.
  • Each competition entry requires its own payment receipt. The same receipt cannot be used for multiple competition entries.
  • Category availability may be subject to minimum participation.
  • The organization reserves the right to combine, adjust, or cancel categories when necessary.
  • Team registration fees are calculated per dancer. Please adjust the quantity according to the number of dancers in your team when completing payment.

FAQ

Do I need a festival pass to compete?
Yes. All competitors must hold a Launch Pass, Dancer Pass, or Full Pass.

Where do I register?
All official competition registrations must be completed through Podium.

How can I confirm my registration?
You must upload a unique payment receipt to Podium for each competition you register for.

Where can I upload my music?
Competition music must be uploaded directly through your Podium registration entry.

Are competition fees refundable?
No. Competition fees are non-refundable.

How do team payments work?
Team Showcase fees are calculated per dancer. Please adjust the quantity according to the number of dancers in your team when completing payment.

Ready to compete?
