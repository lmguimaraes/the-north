Venue

Cirque Éloize

The North Latin Festival 2026 will take place at Cirque Éloize, one of Montreal’s most iconic creative spaces.

Known for its unique event experiences, multimedia performances, and immersive productions, Cirque Éloize embodies the excellence, innovation, and creativity that inspire the spirit of The North.

Located in the heart of the city, this artistic venue will become the home of our first edition, bringing together workshops, competitions, performances, social dancing, and the Latin dance community in a setting Where Cold Meets Fire.

Address Cirque Éloize 417 Rue Berri Montreal, QC H2Y 3E1
